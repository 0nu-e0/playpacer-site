Questions

Why breaks? Isn't a daily limit enough?

A daily limit says how much, not how. With only a limit, a child can spend the whole allowance in one sitting. PlayPacer spreads it out: a stretch of play, a break to move and rest, then more play.

Does break time use up their daily allowance?

No. Break time and time away from the PC don't count. Only time actually using the PC does.

What do I need?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC (64-bit), an administrator account for you, and a separate Windows account for each child. The setup wizard can create the children's accounts for you.

Does it work on phones, tablets or Macs?

Not yet. PlayPacer is for Windows PCs today. A phone app for parents, and limits on kids' phones, are planned as optional add-ons. The Windows app you buy keeps working either way.

Can my child get around it?

PlayPacer is built to be hard to get around on a standard account, and it alerts you to the common tricks. No parental control is perfect, though: a determined, tech-savvy child with physical access to a PC can find a way, so it works best alongside talking with your kids.

What if I don't like it?

Try it free for 14 days first. If you buy it and it's not right for your family, ask for a refund within 30 days. See the refund policy.
