Refund policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

30-day money-back guarantee. If PlayPacer isn't right for your family, ask within 30 days of buying and you'll get a full refund. No need to give a reason.

How to ask for a refund

Email support@playpacer.app from the address you used to buy, with your order number (it's in your receipt email). You can also reply to the receipt itself. We'll process it within 3 working days.

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Lemon Squeezy, our reseller and Merchant of Record. Depending on your bank, it can take 5 to 10 working days to show up.

After a refund

Your license key is deactivated. The rules you set keep working, but you won't be able to change settings until you buy again. To remove PlayPacer completely, uninstall it from Windows Settings → Apps.

After 30 days

Refunds after 30 days are at our discretion, but if something's gone wrong please get in touch anyway. We'd rather fix it.

Subscriptions

Phone subscriptions aren't available yet. When they are, you'll be able to cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the period you've paid for. This page will be updated with the details before they launch.

This policy doesn't affect any rights you have under the law where you live.
