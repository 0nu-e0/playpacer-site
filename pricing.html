Buying questions

Is it really a one-time price?

Yes. The Windows app is yours to keep, with free updates for version 1. The phone add-ons will be optional subscriptions. If you ever stop a subscription, only the phone features stop: the Windows app keeps working.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Limits, breaks and bedtimes keep working exactly as you set them, so nobody is suddenly unrestricted. You just can't change settings until you enter a license key.

How do I get my license key?

It's emailed to you right after you buy, and shown on the order page. Paste it into PlayPacer's Parent Console under Settings → License.

Who processes the payment?

Our order process is handled by our online reseller, Lemon Squeezy, who is the Merchant of Record for all our orders. They handle payment, sales tax and receipts. We never see your card details.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 30 days of buying. See the refund policy.
