Support

Stuck, found a bug, or have an idea? Email support@playpacer.app. A real person reads every message, usually within two working days.

It helps to include your Windows version, what you expected to happen and what happened instead. Please don't send your license key in full.

Common questions

I forgot my parent PIN

Sign in to Windows with your administrator account and open the PlayPacer Parent Console. As an administrator you can set a new PIN under Settings, which also clears any wrong-PIN lockout.

My child is on a break but should be allowed on

On the break screen, choose the parent option and enter your PIN to end the break or add time. You can also do it from the Parent Console on your own account, on the Today page.

Why does my child need their own Windows account?

PlayPacer applies each child's rules to their own account. Your administrator account is never limited, so you can't lock yourself out. The setup wizard can create the children's accounts for you.

Emails aren't arriving

Most email providers, including Gmail, need an "app password" instead of your normal password. Create one in your email account's security settings, enter it in the Parent Console under Settings → Email, then use "Send test email". Check your spam folder too.

Moving to a new PC

Uninstall PlayPacer from the old PC first, then install it on the new one and enter the same license key. If the key says it's already in use, email us and we'll free it up.

How do I uninstall?

From your administrator account, open Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps, find PlayPacer and choose Uninstall. It removes the app, its settings and the browser settings it changed, for every child.
