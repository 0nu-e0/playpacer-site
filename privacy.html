Privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

The short version: PlayPacer keeps your family's information on your own PC. We don't receive your children's names, screen time or browsing history. We only get what's needed to sell you a license: your name, email address and order details.

This policy covers the PlayPacer app for Windows ("PlayPacer", "the app") and this website, playpacer.app. "We" and "us" means PlayPacer, an independent software publisher based in Oregon, USA. Questions: support@playpacer.app.

Information that stays on your PC

To do its job, PlayPacer keeps the following on the PC where it's installed, in a folder only administrators can open:

  • Your settings: each child's Windows account name and the nickname you give them, their limits, breaks and bedtimes, and your parent PIN (stored as a one-way hash, not the PIN itself).
  • Usage: when each child used the PC, for how long, breaks, and events such as a limit being reached or a parent override.
  • Browsing history, if you turn browsing monitoring on: sites visited and searches made in the children's web browsers, copied from those browsers.
  • Your email settings, if you turn email on. Passwords are encrypted with Windows' built-in protection and only work on that PC.
  • Log files used to diagnose problems.

None of this is sent to us. It stays on your PC until you delete it or uninstall PlayPacer, which removes it. PlayPacer doesn't record keystrokes, take screenshots or use the camera or microphone. It shows each child a tray icon and a notice explaining what a parent can see.

What the app sends over the internet

  • License checks. When you enter a license key, and about once a week after that, the app sends the key, a license instance id and your PC's name to our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy, to confirm the license is valid. No information about your children is included.
  • Email you set up. Alerts and weekly summaries are sent through the email account you enter, to the addresses you choose. They go from your email provider to you; we don't receive them.
  • Optional phone connection. An early, optional feature can connect the PC to a phone app through a Google Firebase project that you create and own. It's off unless you set it up, and the data goes to your project, not to us.

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party trackers.

Buying a license

Orders are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our online reseller and Merchant of Record. Lemon Squeezy collects your payment details, billing address and email to process the order and handle taxes; their use of that information is covered by the Lemon Squeezy privacy policy. We receive your name, email address, country, what you bought and your license key, and use them to provide your license, answer support requests and handle refunds. We never see your card details.

Support emails

If you email us, we keep the conversation to help you and to improve PlayPacer. Please don't send us your children's information; we don't need it to help.

This website

playpacer.app uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. It's hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub privacy statement.

Children's privacy

PlayPacer is used by parents and guardians to supervise their own children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children: the information about children described above is entered by the parent and kept on the family's own PC, not sent to us. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. We share it only with the service providers above (Lemon Squeezy for orders and licenses, our email provider for support mail), or when the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep order records as long as needed for your license, and as tax and accounting rules require. You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing support@playpacer.app. Information kept on your PC is under your control: uninstalling PlayPacer deletes it.

Changes

If we change how we handle information (for example when the phone apps launch), we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
